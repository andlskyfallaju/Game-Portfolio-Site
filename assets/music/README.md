# Music Folder

Drop your `.mp3` files in this folder to play them through the in-game Retro Boombox Stereo!

### How It Works:
1. Songs named `track1.mp3`, `track2.mp3`, `track3.mp3`, etc. are automatically recognized by the Boombox.
2. The player automatically reads standard embedded **ID3 tags** (Song Title and Artist) directly from your `.mp3` files and displays them in the track list, cassette label, and status bar!
3. You can also customize track details manually in `src/data/music.js`.
